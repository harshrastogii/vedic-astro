Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

AstroTalk (astrotalk.space) is a free, personal astrology website. It has no ads, no paywalls, and it never sells or shares your data.

What we store

  • Your sign-in details: your name, email address and profile picture, received from Google or GitHub when you sign in. We never see or store your password.
  • Birth profiles you create: name, gender, date, time and place of birth.
  • Your AI chats and AI readings, so you can continue them later.

If you use the site without signing in, profiles are kept only in your own browser.

Who can see it

Only you. Each account's profiles, chats and readings are stored in a database (Supabase) with row-level security, which blocks every account from reading or changing another account's data.

The AI chat

When you ask the AI a question, your message and a text summary of the selected birth chart are sent to an AI model through Vercel AI Gateway to write the answer. They are not used for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can delete any birth profile or chat inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account, contact the site owner through the project page on GitHub: github.com/harshrastogii/vedic-astro.

Cookies

The site stores your sign-in session and theme choice in your browser. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies.
